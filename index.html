Research

My doctoral research is in symbolic computation, with a focus on symbolic integration and summation, creative telescoping, and algorithms for differential and difference forms.

  • Symbolic integration and summation. I develop reduction algorithms for rational functions and investigate how these methods extend to differential and difference forms.
  • Creative telescoping. I study how reduction methods yield telescopers for definite integrals and sums and how residues can explain their minimality.
  • Combinatorial identities. I study the algebraic structure of Wilf–Zeilberger (WZ) pairs and their higher-dimensional analogues, and explore how to use them to discover and prove identities.

I am interested in special functions and q-series, particularly in problems at the intersection of computer algebra and combinatorics.
